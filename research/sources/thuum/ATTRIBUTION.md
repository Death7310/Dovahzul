# Attribution for the Thuum.org dictionary

## What is here

`Dragon_Language_Print_Dictionary_5th_Edition.docx` is the *Dragon Language Dictionary, Dragon-English and English-Dragon, Fifth Edition*, written and published by the Thuum.org community and signed by its administrator. It is a Word conversion of the PDF that Thuum.org offers as a free download:

<https://www.thuum.org/library/Dragon_Language_Print_Dictionary_5th_Edition.pdf>

The project owner supplied this copy on 2026-11-15. SHA-256: `5931c19c1ad89fd9ea9b4fbfeeecdd06bd09715f9828f361fff94c13456bfcf0`.

`research/data/fifth-edition.json` is built from it by `tools/reconcile/parse_fifth_edition.py`.

## Credit

All of the dictionary's own content (word types, pronunciations, expanded definitions, connotations, etymologies, notes, the choice of examples and its own translations) is the work of Thuum.org and its contributors. Anything this project takes from it is credited to Thuum.org.
